Yoga for Beginners 30 Day Plan privacy policy

Applies to Yoga for Beginners 30 Day Plan (package app.yogaworkout.homeyoga) on Google Play and Galaxy Store. Last updated 13 October 2026.

Yoga for Beginners 30 Day Plan ("the app") is published by Zoopet ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. Contact: contact@zoopet.in.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your practice history, plans, routines and settings stay on your phone.
  • Plans, pose instructions, voice guidance (your phone's text-to-speech) and background sounds work offline.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads, which process device and advertising identifiers as described below.
  • The app does not collect health or body measurements, location, contacts, photos or camera data.

Advertising

Unless you buy "Remove ads", the app shows ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which may deliver ads from Unity Ads through mediation. Ads are never shown during a practice session. These services may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, and where required elsewhere, we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are requested; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store. We never see your payment details. The app receives only a purchase record to confirm that ads should be removed.

Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases), notifications (only if you switch reminders on) and "run at start-up" (to restore your reminder after the phone restarts). The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or health permissions.

Health note

The app offers general yoga and stretching guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise routine.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not designed for the Google Play Families programme.

Your data: export and deletion

In Settings you can export your practice history as a CSV file (shared only to the app you choose) and delete all your data from the phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage also deletes everything it stored. Because we do not hold personal data about you on any server, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For questions about data processed by Google or Unity for ads, use the links above, or contact us at contact@zoopet.in.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes, and change the date at the top.
